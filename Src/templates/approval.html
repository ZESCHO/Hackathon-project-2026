🔄 Approval Workflow

1️⃣

Request

A user submits a service request through the platform.

2️⃣

AI Planning

The AI analyzes the request, identifies its intent and category, and determines the required workflow.

3️⃣

Human Approval

An authorized human reviews the AI analysis and approves or rejects the request.

4️⃣

Controlled Execution

Only approved requests can proceed through the controlled execution gate.
